The problem02 / 10

Everyone said it was playing.

MobiumApp's Audio Demo plays two seconds of zeros: a player that is started, and silent.

The app's own line says playing. Android's audio service says the player is started — exactly what it says for a tone.

Neither can tell you whether anything was heard. Only the sound can.

Pixel 7 emulator, Android 15
Hear it03 / 10

A timeline, not a waveform.

$ mobium audio start
$ mobium tap testid=audioSequence
$ mobium audio stop -o sequence.wav

Sound and silence to a tenth of a second, each sound's pitch and level, read from the emulator's own audio. Nothing is installed on the device.

Assert it04 / 10

Pitch and length, in order.

$ mobium audio stop -o seq.wav --expect 440:1.8-2.3,880:1.8-2.3
saved seq.wav: … 440 Hz … 880 Hz …

$ mobium audio stop -o tone.wav --expect 880
error: expected 880 Hz; heard 440 Hz for 2.1s
(0.3–2.4s) — the capture is saved at tone.wav

A wrong expectation fails, says what was heard, and keeps the capture as the evidence. --expect silence asserts nothing was heard; a tap's click, a tenth of a second, does not count.

The volume06 / 10

What arrives follows the volume.

The same tone, at four media volumes:

Media volumeHeard at
15 of 15-9 dBFS
5 of 15-42 dBFS
1 of 15-62 dBFS
0 of 15silence

So a test asserts pitch and length, never level — and every answer says the volume it was taken at: “at media volume 0 of 15, where nothing played as media is heard”.

Any interruption08 / 10

Any sound that cuts across the app.

The app mutedfor a call, or by the device's or its own volume — with Android's reason.
Another app's sound over ita ringtone, an alarm, a notification, a voice call, the assistant, navigation, another app's media — each named by what it is for.
A Clock timer's alarm over the toneemulator: an alarm played 4.4–6.7 s
Pixel 8 Pro: an alarm played 5.3–10.5 s

Read from Android's audio service. A touch's click is not an interruption.

On a real phone09 / 10

A phone's sound stays on the phone.

Nothing outside a phone hears what it plays. On an Android phone a capture records what interrupted the app instead — the same answer, with nothing installed:

$ mobium audio stop
its sound is not captured on a phone;
interrupted: an alarm played 5.3–10.5 s
Any phone adb reachesa device farm's included. Making one ring takes a call from outside, which only a farm with a line can place.
An emulator hears everythingand rings on demand, with nothing else to set up.
